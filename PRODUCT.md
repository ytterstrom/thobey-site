# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Direct buyers**: IT and development managers or CTOs at Swedish companies and public agencies who need an architect, a tech lead, or help getting started with AI in software development.
- **Consulting brokers**: agencies that place consultants on assignments and need to judge credibility and fit quickly.

Both arrive to answer the same question: is Thomas the right senior person for this assignment, and how do I reach him?

## Product Purpose

The personal consulting site for Thobey Solutions, the one-person firm of Thomas Ytterström, system architect, tech lead and developer in Habo, Sweden. It presents two offers (architecture and technical leadership; AI in software development), backs them with real work and background, and turns interest into an email or call. Success is a qualified inquiry from a buyer or broker.

## Positioning

- **An architect who still codes.** In IT since 2003, designs the solution and leads the team that builds it, and stays hands-on in C#/.NET, TypeScript and React.
- **AI in development, in practice.** Drives AI-assisted development (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, MCP integrations) as part of a company-wide AI super user group, not as theory.
- **Business-minded.** Informatics background: technology as a way to move the business forward, not an end in itself.

## Operating Context

- Visitors usually come from a LinkedIn profile, a broker's shortlist, or a direct referral, and skim to confirm seniority, stack and availability.
- Contact happens by email (info@thobey.se, with a topic-prefilled subject), phone, or LinkedIn. There is no form or backend.
- Work is on-site or remote across Sweden, mainly on the Microsoft/.NET and Azure stack, with React and Node where they fit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: plain HTML, CSS and a small `site.js`, with no build dependencies. Pages, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are generated by `tools/build-taste.py` from its copy dictionary; `styles.css`, `site.js`, fonts and images are hand-written. Copy changes go in the generator, not the HTML.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages for now; the final domain is thobey.se (the generator writes a CNAME for it).
- English (`/`) and Swedish (`/sv/`) ship together with the same content, always.
- Open: whether the current logo is final, and whether a formal accessibility standard is required.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Thobey Solutions (Org. nr 559197-7367), presented through Thomas Ytterström personally. First-person voice ("I help…").
- Voice: plain, concrete, confident without hype; curious and solution-focused.

## Evidence on Hand

- Selected work: Husqvarna Service Hub (45,000+ dealers worldwide; built since 2019, leads its six-person team), Footly (1,500+ portal users; replaced several full-time roles of manual work), Medster (4,000+ healthcare learners).
- Organizations worked with: Husqvarna, Swedish National Courts Administration, Government Offices of Sweden, Volvo, SAAB, Chalmers, Volkswagen Finans, Swedish Board of Agriculture.
- Background: MSc Informatics (Jönköping International Business School); Certified Software Architect, Microsoft Platform; in IT since 2003; volleyball referee at the highest national level.
- Absent: no testimonials, client quotes, case-study write-ups, pricing or availability dates. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Prove seniority with real, named work and numbers, never adjectives.
2. Make the hands-on credibility visible: the person who designs it also builds it.
3. Serve the skimming broker and the deliberate buyer on the same page; the key facts must survive a ten-second scan.
4. One short path to contact, in either language.
5. Keep it light enough to stay static and maintainable from one generator script.
